# Northwind Information Security Policy

A fictional security policy used as sample data for DocuMind AI.

## Password Policy

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all administrator accounts. An account is locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed login attempts.

## Phishing

Any suspected phishing email must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious messages. The security team runs simulated phishing tests every quarter.

## Incident Response

The incident response process has six phases: Preparation, Identification, Containment, Eradication, Recovery and Lessons Learned. A priority 1 incident requires a response within 15 minutes. A priority 2 incident requires a response within 2 hours. A post-incident review must be completed within 5 working days.

## Data Classification

Data is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer payment details, must be encrypted at rest and in transit and may only be accessed by named individuals.

## Backups

Daily incremental backups and weekly full backups are performed. Backups are retained for 35 days. Restore tests are performed every quarter.

## Patching and Devices

Critical security patches must be installed within 72 hours of release. All company laptops require full-disk encryption. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team immediately.
