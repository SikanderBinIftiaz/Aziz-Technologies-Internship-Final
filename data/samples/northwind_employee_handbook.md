# Northwind Technologies Employee Handbook

Northwind Technologies is a fictional software company used as sample data for DocuMind AI.

## Working Hours

The standard work week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, when all employees are expected to be available for meetings. Outside core hours, employees may arrange flexible start and finish times with their manager.

## Annual Leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Leave of more than 3 consecutive days must be requested at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive sick days. Unused sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental Leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must be started within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. New hires are expected to work on site for their first 4 weeks. Northwind provides a one-time home-office stipend of $400 and a monthly internet allowance of $40 for remote employees.

## Expense Policy

Meals while traveling on business are reimbursed up to $60 per day. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Receipts are mandatory for every expense of $25 or more. Flights shorter than 6 hours must be booked in economy class.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has a learning budget of $1,200 per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Managers approve requests that relate to the employee's role.

## Probation and Notice

New employees complete a probation period of 3 months. After probation, the notice period for resignation is 30 days.
