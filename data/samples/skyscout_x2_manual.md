# SkyScout X2 Drone User Manual

The SkyScout X2 is a fictional consumer camera drone used as sample data for DocuMind AI.

## Specifications

The SkyScout X2 weighs 895 grams. It reaches a maximum speed of 68 km/h and a maximum transmission range of 5 km. The default maximum altitude is 120 meters. The drone is resistant to wind up to 10.7 meters per second, which is Level 5 on the Beaufort scale. The operating temperature range is 0 to 40 degrees Celsius.

## Camera

The camera has a 20 megapixel sensor and records 4K video at up to 60 frames per second. The gimbal stabilizes the camera on three axes. Footage is saved to a microSD card of up to 512 GB.

## Battery and Charging

The intelligent flight battery has a capacity of 5000 mAh and provides up to 32 minutes of flight time. A full charge takes about 75 minutes using the included 65 W charger. Store the battery at 40 to 60 percent charge if it will not be used for more than 10 days.

## Return to Home

The drone automatically returns to its take-off point when the battery level drops to 20 percent, when the control signal is lost for more than 6 seconds, or when the pilot presses the Return to Home button. Before each flight, confirm that the home point was recorded.

## Status Lights

A solid green light means GPS is locked and the drone is ready. A flashing yellow light means there is no GPS signal. A flashing red light means the battery is low. A solid red light indicates a critical error and the drone must land immediately.

## Maintenance

Inspect the propellers before every flight and replace any that are chipped or bent. Clean the camera lens with a microfiber cloth. Update the firmware through the SkyScout app whenever a new version is announced; keep the battery above 40 percent during an update.

## Safety and Regulations

Do not fly within 5 km of an airport. Drones heavier than 250 grams must be registered with the national aviation authority before use. Always keep the drone within visual line of sight.
